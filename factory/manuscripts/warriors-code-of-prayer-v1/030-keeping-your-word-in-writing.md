# Keeping Your Word In Writing

> Lying lips are an abomination to Yahweh, but those who do the truth are his delight. -- Proverbs 12:22

Your integrity in this profession will mostly be decided in paperwork.

Not in a dramatic moment. In a report you wrote at 0430 on your sixth day, when you were smoked and the easy version was two sentences shorter than the accurate one. In a training log where you rounded four hours to eight. In a statement where you left out the part that makes you look slow.

Nobody will ever catch most of it. That is exactly the point.

Documents in this work outlive the men who write them. A report you filed in 2019 can surface in a proceeding in 2031, read out loud by somebody who does not know you, in a room where you do not get to explain the context. What is on the page is what happened, as far as that room is concerned.

The proverb splits people by what they do rather than by what they feel about honesty. Doing the truth. It is an activity. It is the man who goes back and corrects a document that nobody flagged, at 0500, when he could have gone home.

There is a second-order effect that men underestimate. The habit of small written inaccuracy erodes your own memory of events. Write it sloppy enough times and you no longer have an accurate record of your own career. You start believing your own smoothed version, and then you cannot learn from the real one.

This is also how you protect other people. Your log is the only thing that can defend the new guy in three years when somebody comes after him for something he did right.

## THE DRILL

Pull up the last report you wrote. Read it as if you were a hostile attorney with time and no relationship to you. Find one sentence that is technically true and functionally misleading, and fix it. If there is nothing to fix, you have a baseline. If there is something, you now know your actual standard rather than your intended one.

::: prayer
Yahweh, my name is on documents I wrote tired and I did not always tell the whole shape of it. Make me a man who does the truth in writing when nobody will ever check. Give me the stomach to go back and correct the one I am thinking about. Amen.
