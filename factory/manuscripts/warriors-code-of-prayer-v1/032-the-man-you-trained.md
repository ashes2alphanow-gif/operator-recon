# The Man You Trained

> The things which you have heard from me, commit to faithful men who will be able to teach others also. -- 2 Timothy 2:2

Nothing you personally accomplish in this career will outlast the men you taught.

Count it honestly. Your case numbers get archived. Your awards go in a box in the garage. The unit you built gets reorganized by somebody who was not there when you built it, probably within four years of your departure.

What does not get reorganized is the agent who runs a track the way you showed him in 2018, and who is now showing somebody else, and who says "an old guy taught me this" without remembering your name.

That is the only compounding asset in this profession. Everything else is linear. You work an hour, you get an hour of output. Teaching is the one thing that multiplies, because the man you taught teaches two more and you are not involved in any of it.

Paul writes to Timothy with four generations in a single sentence. What you heard from me, commit to faithful men, who will teach others. Paul, Timothy, faithful men, others. He is describing a transmission chain and he is explicitly not describing a personal legacy, because by the third link nobody is thinking about Paul.

Notice the filter. Faithful men. Not the most talented. Not the ones who make you look good. Faithful, meaning the ones who will actually carry it and hand it on rather than collect it.

Most senior men in this work hold their knowledge tighter than they should. Some of it is time. Some of it is that the knowledge is the last thing that makes them necessary. That is understandable and it is still a waste, because knowledge held is knowledge that dies on your retirement date.

## THE DRILL

Pick one thing you know that is not written down anywhere and would leave with you. A way of reading ground. A way of talking to a family at the worst moment of their life. Write one page on it this week. Not a manual. One page. Then hand it to one man and make him teach it back to you.

::: prayer
God, most of what I know is going to walk out the door with me and I have been holding it because it makes me necessary. Show me the faithful man on my crew. Give me one page this week and the humility to hand it over. Amen.
:::
