# Teaching a Boy to Stand

> Train up a child in the way he should go, and when he is old he will not depart from it. -- Proverbs 22:6

He is not listening to your advice. He is watching your Tuesday.

Every father in this profession has a speech he has given about hard work, or about telling the truth, or about finishing what you start. Most of us gave that speech in the truck with the boy strapped in behind us, and it was a good speech, and it moved almost nothing.

What moves him is watching you come home smoked after fourteen hours and still stand up to do the thing you said you would do. What moves him is hearing you on the phone with somebody who is being unreasonable, and hearing you stay level. What moves him is watching how you talk about a supervisor you do not respect when you think only family is in the room.

He is running a continuous observation on you and filing it under what a man does. Not what a man says. What a man does when he is tired, angry, or caught being wrong.

That proverb has been misread as a guarantee by about four generations of anxious parents, and used as a stick by others. Read it as what it is, a proverb about the deep grain of training. The word carries the sense of dedicating something toward its direction. What you install early sets a direction, and direction is durable even when behavior is not.

Which means the training is happening right now whether or not you designed it. You are installing something this week. The question is only whether you chose it.

The most transferable thing you own is not your job. It is the ability to keep a commitment when nothing in your body wants to.

## THE DRILL

Pick one thing you want him to be able to do at thirty. Then do it visibly, in front of him, this week, and name it out loud once while you are doing it. Not a lecture. One sentence. "I am going because I said I would, not because I want to." Then let him watch the rest.

God, he is copying me and not listening to me, and some of what he is copying I did not choose. Make my Tuesday worth imitating. Let him see me keep one thing this week that I did not feel like keeping. Amen.
