# Walking In the Door

> As for me and my house, we will serve Yahweh. -- Joshua 24:15

The first ninety seconds decide the next twelve hours.

You know this. You have watched it go both ways in your own kitchen. There is a version where you come in, set the bag down, find whoever is awake, and put a hand on a shoulder before you say a word. And there is a version where you come in scanning, see the dishes, and open with a question that is technically about dishes.

Same man, same house, same day. Different first ninety seconds, completely different evening.

The problem is that you are still running the posture the job requires. On shift you scan for what is wrong, because that is the entire skill. Find the anomaly. Notice the thing out of place. You have spent years building that until it runs without being asked for.

Then you carry it through your own front door and point it at people who are not a threat and are not an assignment.

Joshua makes his declaration at the end of a long career of hard movement. He is old. He has done the work. And the thing he stakes out is not a battlefield, it is a household. He names his house as the unit he is responsible for and the thing he will be accountable for.

You are the supervisor of that unit. Nobody else is going to set the tone of it, and the tone gets set in the doorway.

This is not about being soft when you get home. It is about switching from a scanning posture to a presence posture, which is a different skill and one you have never practiced deliberately once.

## THE DRILL

Build a threshold drill and run it every day for two weeks. Stop at the door before you open it. Say one sentence in your head about who is on the other side and what they need from you. Then go in and find a person before you find a problem. Hand on a shoulder, name said out loud. The dishes will still be there in ten minutes.

::: prayer
God, I bring the wrong posture through my own door and my family has learned to read it before I say anything. Let me find a person before I find a problem tonight. This house is the unit I answer for. Amen.
:::
