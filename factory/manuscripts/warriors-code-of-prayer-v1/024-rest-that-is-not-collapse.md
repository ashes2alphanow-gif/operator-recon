# Rest That Is Not Collapse

> You shall labor six days, and do all your work. -- Exodus 20:9

Most men in this line do not rest. They collapse, recover just enough to be functional, and call that a day off.

You know the shape of it. You come off a stretch, you sleep until 1100, you are useless until about 1500, and then you burn what is left on errands that piled up. Monday you go back in having been off duty for two days and rested for none of them.

Collapse is what happens to the leftover hours. Rest is a thing you schedule and defend.

Notice which half of the commandment gets quoted. Everybody knows the sabbath half. Almost nobody quotes the first half, which is an instruction to work six days and do all your work. The command has two edges. Do the work, all of it, and then genuinely stop. Men in this profession are excellent at the first edge and treat the second as a suggestion for people with easier jobs.

Here is why it is not soft. An unrested man makes worse decisions and cannot tell that he is making them. That is the specific danger of fatigue, that the instrument measuring your judgment is the thing that is degraded. You are not the exception. Nobody who has ever said they were the exception was the exception.

Rest also has to have content. A day off spent on the phone reading about things that make you angry is not rest, it is a different kind of work with no pay. Real rest for most men is physical, outdoors, with other people, and away from the thing that pays them.

Your schedule is genuinely hard. That is a reason to plan rest deliberately, not a reason to skip it.

## THE DRILL

Take the next fourteen days and find one block of six hours that you will protect like a court date. Put one thing in it that is not a chore and not a screen. Tell one person it is happening so it costs something to cancel. Six hours, planned, defended. Not the leftovers.

::: prayer
God, I collapse and I call it rest and then I wonder why nothing refills. You commanded the work and then commanded the stop, and I have been treating half of that as optional. Show me the six hours and give me the spine to defend them. Amen.
:::
