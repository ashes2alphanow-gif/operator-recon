# The New Guy

> Let no man despise your youth, but be an example to those who believe, in word, in your way of life, in love, in faith. -- 1 Timothy 4:12

How the newest man gets treated is the actual standard. Everything else is a poster.

You can read a station in twenty minutes by watching what happens to the guy with eleven months on. Does anybody explain the reason behind a procedure, or does he just get told. Does he get corrected in front of people, or off to the side. Does anybody eat lunch with him.

The written standards do not matter much next to that. The new guy is learning the real culture, and he is learning it fast, because a man that new is absorbing everything and cannot yet tell the difference between what is doctrine and what is one senior guy's bad habit.

Five years from now he will do it the way he saw it done in month eleven. That is the whole transmission mechanism. Not the academy. Not the policy manual.

Paul writes to a young leader who is getting dismissed because of his age, and the instruction is not to demand respect. It is to be the example, in word and in way of life, and let that settle the question. That cuts both directions. If you are the young man, the answer is your conduct, not your argument. If you are the senior man, then his conduct is downstream of yours whether or not you signed up for that.

There is a specific failure worth naming. The senior guy who is technically excellent and quietly cruel. He is competent, so nobody corrects him, and he teaches an entire generation that competence buys you the right to treat people badly.

Do not be him. And when you are in the room with him and he starts on the new guy, the silence of every other senior man is the thing the new guy actually remembers.

## THE DRILL

Find the newest man on your crew this week. Give him one piece of the reasoning behind something he has only been told to do. Not the rule. The reason, and the story of what happened to somebody when it was not followed. Ten minutes. That is how doctrine gets transferred.
