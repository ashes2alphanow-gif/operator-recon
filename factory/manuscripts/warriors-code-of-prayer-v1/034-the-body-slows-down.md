# The Body Slows Down

> Though our outward man is decaying, yet our inward man is renewed day by day. -- 2 Corinthians 4:16

There is a day when you cannot do what you could do at twenty-five, and you find out about it in front of other people.

It is a hill you used to take without thinking. It is the young guy pulling away from you on a track and looking back to see where you are. It is your knee after a movement that would not have registered fifteen years ago. And the physical part is not what wrecks men. It is what the moment says about the arc.

Most men handle it in one of two bad ways. Some deny it, train through an injury that needed six weeks, and turn a manageable decline into a medical retirement. Others quit early and get soft at forty-four, decide the physical part of their identity is over, and lose ten years they could have had.

Paul does not do either. He says plainly that the outward man is wasting away, with no argument and no spin. That is a straight acknowledgment from a man who took more physical damage than most people reading this ever will. And then he sets the other thing beside it. The inward man is renewed day by day.

Two things are true at once. The decline is real, and it is not the whole account.

Practically, this is a transition in what you contribute, and the men who navigate it well start planning it around year twelve instead of discovering it at year nineteen. You stop being the man who runs the hill fastest and become the man who reads the ground, sets the plan, and keeps four young guys from doing something stupid on the far side of it.

That is not a demotion. It is the only path that leads anywhere except out.

## THE DRILL

Name the specific physical thing you have lost, out loud, without arguing about it. Then name one thing you can do now that you could not do at twenty-five. Judgment, reading people, staying calm, knowing when not to go. Write both. Then adjust your training for the body you have rather than the one you are mourning.

::: prayer
God, I am slower and it happened while I was not paying attention. Keep me from wrecking myself proving otherwise and from quitting early out of pride. Renew the part of me that is not on the clock. Amen.
