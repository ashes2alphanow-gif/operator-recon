# Leading Tired Men

> Those who wait for Yahweh will renew their strength. They will run and not be weary. They will walk and not faint. -- Isaiah 40:31

On hour fourteen, leadership is almost entirely about lowering the noise.

Early in a long operation you can lead with energy. Men will follow intensity for about six hours. After that, intensity from the supervisor reads as panic, and tired men hearing panic start making their own plans, and now you have four independent plans running on one net.

What tired men need is different and it is unglamorous. They need the next task, stated in one sentence. They need to know how long. They need somebody to tell them when to eat and to mean it. And they need to see that the man in charge is not deteriorating faster than they are.

That is it. That is the whole toolkit at hour fourteen.

Read the verse in order, because the order is the point. Mount up with wings. Run and not be weary. Walk and not faint. It descends from soaring to running to walking, which is not a downgrade, it is the actual arc of a long operation. The last and hardest promise is the walking one. Anybody can be inspired. Continuing to walk when there is nothing left is the thing that requires supplied strength.

Most leadership writing sells you the eagle. The job is almost entirely the walk.

So when you are running men late into something, stop trying to generate a moment. Bring the noise down. Shorten the sentences. Give the next thing, the duration, and the food plan. Then be visibly steady, because twenty tired men are reading your face for information about how bad this is.

## THE DRILL

Next time you are past hour twelve with men under you, do three things in the first sixty seconds of contact. Name the next task in one sentence. Give a time. Tell them the next food or water point. Then stop talking. Watch what it does to the net.

::: prayer
God, I am leading men who are past empty and I am close behind them. Take the noise out of my voice. Give me the next sentence, short and clear. Let them see somebody steady when they look at me. Amen.
:::
