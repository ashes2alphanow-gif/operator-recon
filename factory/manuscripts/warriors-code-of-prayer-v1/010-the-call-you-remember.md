# The Call You Remember

> Yahweh is near to those who have a broken heart, and saves those who have a crushed spirit. -- Psalm 34:18

Every man in this work has one on top of the stack.

Not the worst one by any objective measure. The one that stuck. Sometimes it is not even the call, it is one detail from it. A shoe. A phone that kept ringing. The way somebody said a name.

You have a version of it that you tell. It is about ninety seconds long, you have told it maybe six times, and it has been sanded smooth by the telling. That version is not the problem. The problem is the other one, the one you have never said out loud, that shows up at 0300 about four times a year and runs unedited.

Men treat that as a malfunction. It is not. It is a normal human nervous system holding something it was not built to file.

The psalm says God is near to the crushed. Not impressed by them, not waiting for them to recover first. Near. That word was chosen carefully. It does not promise the memory goes away. It locates someone next to you while you have it.

Here is the part most men get wrong. They think the goal is to stop having it. So they measure their progress by whether it came back, and every return feels like failure, and the failure gets added to the pile.

Change the metric. The goal is not silence. The goal is that when it comes back you are not alone in the room with it, and that you can say it to one human being who will not flinch.

If it has moved past memory into something that is running your life, a peer support number, a chaplain, or a clinician who works with first responders is not weakness. It is the same as calling for a second unit.

## THE DRILL

Say your unedited version out loud one time this week. Not the ninety-second version. To God in a parked truck, or to one man who has stood in the same places you have. Once. Then stop. You are not trying to process it tonight, you are breaking the seal on it never having been said.

::: prayer
God, there is a thing from that night I have never said out loud, not once, not even to you. You say you are near to the crushed. Be near for the length of one sentence while I say it. Amen.
:::
