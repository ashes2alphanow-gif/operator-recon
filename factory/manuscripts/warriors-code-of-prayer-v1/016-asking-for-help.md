# Asking for Help

> Bear one another's burdens, and so fulfill the law of Christ. -- Galatians 6:2

You will call for a second unit without a second thought.

You have requested air support, asked for a translator, ordered a medevac, and called a colleague at 0200 about a warrant question. You do all of that instantly, without ego, because the mission requires it and because you are competent enough to know what you cannot do alone.

Then you get home and you will not tell your wife you are struggling.

That is not consistency. That is a category error. You treat operational help as tactical and personal help as confession, and only one of them feels like an admission of failure. Both are the same act. Both are a man reporting an accurate picture of his capability against the task.

Paul writes to carry each other's loads, and he calls that the fulfillment of Christ's law, which is heavy language for a practical instruction. Note the direction he assumes. It runs both ways. A man who only ever carries for others and never lets anybody carry for him is not obeying that verse. He is violating half of it and taking credit for the other half.

There is a man on your crew right now who would take your call and never mention it again. You know exactly which one. You have been not calling him for about two years.

The reason is not pride, usually. It is arithmetic. You have decided your problem would cost more to hand over than to carry. That arithmetic is almost always wrong, and it is the same arithmetic that gets men to walk into a building alone.

If the load has moved past hard into dangerous, call 988 in the United States, or your agency peer support line. Tonight, not after this rotation.

## THE DRILL

Text one man today. Not a status update. One true sentence about where you actually are, with no joke attached to soften it. "I am not doing well and I have not said that to anybody." Send it before you overthink the wording. Then answer the phone when it rings.

::: prayer
Lord, I will call for anything the mission needs and nothing that I need. You made carrying each other a law and I have been obeying half of it for years. Give me the nerve to send one honest sentence to one man tonight. Amen.
:::
