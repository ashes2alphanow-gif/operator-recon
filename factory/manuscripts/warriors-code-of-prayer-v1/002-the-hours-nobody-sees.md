# The Hours Nobody Sees

> Pray to your Father who is in secret, and your Father who sees in secret will reward you openly. -- Matthew 6:6

Nobody is going to know about tonight.

There is no report for the fence you checked that was fine. No award for the four hours you spent on a sensor hit that turned out to be cattle. The county will never learn the name of the man who sat at the intersection until 0400 so that nothing happened there. When they do a story about your agency, they will use a photo of somebody in a helicopter.

Men rot in that. Not fast. Slow, over about eight years, the way a fence post rots at the ground line while the top of it still looks fine.

Here is the thing worth knowing. The verse does not say your unseen work is fine because someday you will be recognized. It says something harder and better. It says the secret place is where the Father already is. He is not waiting at the awards ceremony. He is in the truck at 0400 with the heater rattling.

That changes the arithmetic of a boring night. If the only value of the shift is what other people find out about, then most of your career is waste with a few good stories in it. If the value of the shift is that it was done right in front of God, then the ratio flips. The seen work is the small part. The rest of it, the part nobody will ever hear about, is the actual body of your life, and it counted while it was happening.

You already believe this about other men. You know which guys on your crew do the work when the supervisor is off the schedule. You respect them more than the ones who perform. Extend yourself the same reading.

## THE DRILL

Pick one thing tonight that nobody will ever check. The tire pressure. The back door of the sector building. A welfare call on a guy you have no reason to check on. Do it properly and tell no one. Not your wife, not the crew. Let it stay in the secret place where it is already counted.

::: prayer
Father, most of what I do disappears. I want credit and I am tired of wanting it. You were in the truck at four in the morning and I did not notice. Let the unseen hours be enough, because they are almost all of them. Amen.
:::
