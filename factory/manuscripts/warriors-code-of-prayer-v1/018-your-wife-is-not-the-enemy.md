# Your Wife Is Not the Enemy

> Husbands, love your wives, even as Christ also loved the assembly and gave himself up for it. -- Ephesians 5:25

She said you have been distant and you heard it as an accusation.

So you defended. You listed the hours, the overtime, the fact that you have not had a full weekend since June, and every item on that list was true. And she was not making a case against you in the first place. She was reporting a condition, and you answered a report with a defense, which told her that raising it costs something.

Do that about forty times and she stops raising it. That silence is not peace. That is a person who has learned the topic is closed.

Here is the mechanism nobody names. You spend your working hours in an adversarial posture. Somebody is lying to you, somebody is testing you, somebody wants something from you and is willing to work an angle to get it. You get good at detecting that. You have to.

Then she says one sentence with an edge in it and the detection system fires on the closest human being.

Paul does not tell husbands to lead, provide, or hold the line, all of which you would find easier. He tells them to love the way Christ loved the church, and then defines it as giving himself up. The model is a man laying something down, not a man holding something.

For you, what gets laid down is the posture. The readiness to be right. The eleven-item list of everything you did this month that proves you are not the problem.

She is not the enemy. She is the one person in your life who has no tactical reason to lie to you about how you are doing, which makes her report the most reliable intelligence you have access to.

## THE DRILL

Next time she raises something, say nothing for five seconds and then ask one question instead of giving one answer. "How long has it been like that?" Then let her talk without correcting a single fact, even the ones that are wrong. You are not conceding the case. You are collecting the report.
