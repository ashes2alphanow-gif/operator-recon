# Retirement Is a Deployment

> The plans of the diligent surely lead to profit, and everyone who is hasty surely rushes to poverty. -- Proverbs 21:5

You would never move a team without a plan, and you are planning to leave this career with a date and a vague feeling.

Ask a man in year eighteen what his plan is. You will get a number and a mood. The number is the retirement date. The mood is some version of taking it easy for a while. That is not a plan, that is a departure time with nothing on the other end of it, and this profession is full of men who found that out the hard way.

Run it the way you would run any other movement. What is the objective. What is the timeline. What has to be in place before execution. What are the contingencies.

The specifics are yours to work out with people who know your actual numbers, and this book is not the place for that. A devotional has no business telling you what your finances should look like. But the discipline is the same discipline you already have, and you are simply not applying it here.

The proverb is about pace as much as it is about planning. The diligent plan and the hasty rush, and the distinction is time, not intelligence. A plan made at year twelve has room in it. A plan made at month three of terminal leave is a reaction.

There is a second piece that men skip entirely because it is harder to write on a calendar. What are you going to do on Tuesday. Not financially. Structurally. Twenty years of shift work built your entire week, your friendships, and your sense of being needed, and all three of those end on the same afternoon.

Men who do well at this have something already running before they walk out. A trade, a business, a role, a place they are expected to be.

## THE DRILL

Put three dates on paper. The date you leave. A date two years before it, which is when the plan has to be in writing and reviewed by somebody qualified. And a date thirty days after you leave, with an actual commitment on it that somebody else is counting on you for. Then tell your wife all three.

::: prayer
God, I have a date and a feeling and I have called that a plan. Give me the diligence to write it down early and the humility to have somebody who knows the details look at it. Show me what I am walking toward, not just what I am walking out of. Amen.
:::
