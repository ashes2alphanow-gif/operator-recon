# The Guilt You Kept

> If we confess our sins, he is faithful and righteous to forgive us, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness. -- 1 John 1:9

You have made decisions in four seconds that other people have spent months reviewing.

That asymmetry is the specific cruelty of this work. A board sits in a room with coffee and a whiteboard and pulls apart something you did with partial information, at night, while your heart rate was at 160 and somebody was screaming. Then you spend the next three years running your own review, except your review never adjourns and never issues a finding.

There are two different things in that pile and you have to separate them, because they need opposite treatment.

The first is regret. You did the best available thing with what you had and the outcome was still bad. That is not sin and confession will not touch it, because there is nothing to forgive. What that needs is a verdict from somebody qualified to give one, and the willingness to accept it.

The second is actual guilt. You did something wrong. You know exactly what it was. You have never said it plainly to anybody, and you have built about nine years of justification around it.

For the second one, the verse is precise and it is a legal statement. Confess, and he is faithful and righteous to forgive. Not likely to. Not if you feel bad enough for long enough. Faithful and righteous, which means that a confessed sin being held against you would be a violation of his own character.

Most men are carrying the first kind and treating it as the second, or carrying the second and calling it the first. Do the sort first. The treatment is different.

## THE DRILL

Write the incident down in one paragraph, factually, the way you would write a statement. Then write two headers under it: what I did wrong, and what was outside my control. Put every sentence under one of them. The second list gets handed off. The first list gets confessed out loud, tonight, by name.

::: prayer
Father, I have been running the same review for years and it never closes. Here is the part that was actually mine, said plainly and without the arguments I have been building around it. You called yourself faithful and righteous about this. I am taking you at your word and closing the file. Amen.
:::
