# Keeping One Promise

> Let your "Yes" be "Yes" and your "No" be "No." -- Matthew 5:37

The damage is not done by the big broken promise. It is done by the small ones, at volume.

You said you would look at the bike. You said you would call your mother back. You said Saturday. None of those were lies when you said them. Every one of them was a real intention issued by a tired man who wanted to give somebody something in the moment and had not looked at the week.

Over a couple of years, the people in your house build a model of you. In that model, what you say has a reliability rating, and it is lower than you think. They stop planning around your yes. They wait to see.

That is a far worse injury than a missed weekend, and it is entirely inside your control, which the schedule is not.

Christ's instruction is aimed at men who over-swore. In that culture people stacked oaths to make a promise sound more binding, and he says cut all of it and let a plain yes carry the full weight. That only works if your yes is expensive. If you hand out yes freely because it is the fastest way to end a conversation, you have made your own word cheap and then wondered why nobody spends it.

The fix is not to promise more carefully in general. It is to reduce volume and raise reliability. One yes that is real beats four that are hopeful.

And when you break one, which you will, the repair is specific. Name it without excuses, say what you are doing instead, and do that thing. Not "sorry, work got crazy." That is a weather report, not a repair.

## THE DRILL

For one week, say no or say a specific time. Nothing in between. "I cannot" is a complete answer. "Saturday at 10" is a complete answer. "I will try to get to it" is now off the table. Count how many times you almost used the old phrase. That count is the size of the problem.

::: prayer
God, my word has gotten cheap in my own house and I did it one small yes at a time. Teach me to say no plainly and to make my yes expensive. Let me keep the next one exactly as I said it. Amen.
:::
