# The Kid Who Waited Up

> You shall teach them diligently to your children, and shall talk of them when you sit in your house, and when you walk by the way. -- Deuteronomy 6:7

He waited up again and you told him to go to bed.

You were right. It was 2340, he has school, and he has done this four nights this week. You handled it correctly and you were tired and you were not wrong.

He is also nine, and what he has figured out is that the only window where he gets you alone is the one where you come in the door. So he keeps engineering it, and getting sent to bed, and engineering it again, because a kid will take negative contact over no contact every time.

Presence is not measured in intent. It is measured in minutes given, which is a brutal metric and the only honest one. You intend to be a present father. You would put your body between that boy and anything. That is real and it is not the same as time, and children cannot read intent. They can only read attendance.

Look at what Moses describes. Sitting in the house. Walking by the way. Lying down and rising up. It is not a program. There is no curriculum and no scheduled block. It is the ordinary hours of a normal day used deliberately, which means the transfer of everything you believe happens in the slots you keep thinking of as leftover time.

You do not have a lot of those slots. That is a real constraint and it is not going away while you are working this schedule. So the slots you do have have to be actually given, not given while looking at a phone.

Fifteen honest minutes beats a Saturday you spent physically present and mentally back on the Thursday incident.

## THE DRILL

Pick one recurring slot that already exists. The drive to practice. The ten minutes before he sleeps on your days off. Breakfast on the two mornings you are home. Name it, defend it, and put the phone in another room during it. One slot, held for a month, beats a new tradition you will not sustain past three weeks.

::: prayer
Father, my son keeps making chances to be near me and I keep sending him to bed for good reasons. Show me the slot I already have. Give me the discipline to put the phone down inside it. Amen.
:::
