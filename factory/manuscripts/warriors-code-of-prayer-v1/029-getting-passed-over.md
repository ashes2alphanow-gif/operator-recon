# Getting Passed Over

> Neither from the east, nor from the west, nor from the south, comes exaltation. But God is the judge. He puts down one and lifts up another. -- Psalm 75:6

The list came out and your name was not on it.

You already know who did make it and you already have a theory about why. Some of that theory is accurate. Boards are run by people, people have preferences, and sometimes the selection is genuinely about who was visible to the right man at the right time rather than who can do the work.

Sit with the accurate part for a second, because pretending the system is perfectly fair is its own kind of weakness. Then get to the part that matters, which is what this does to you over the next six months.

There are two ways men handle this and only one of them survives.

The first is to take the list as a verdict. He internalizes it. He decides that the organization has made an assessment of his worth and that the assessment is correct, and something goes out of the way he works. You have watched this happen to somebody. He is still there, doing the minimum, twelve years later.

The second is to take the list as data about a process. It tells you about this board, in this cycle, under these criteria. It does not tell you what you are worth, because a promotion board has no instrument that measures that.

The psalm makes the same distinction with more authority behind it. Exaltation does not come from any direction on the compass. It is not distributed by geography or by who you know in a region. God is the judge. That is either a cold statement or a liberating one depending on whether you actually wanted the list to be the final word on you.

## THE DRILL

Write down two lists. What this board actually told you about how you are seen, and what you can change before the next cycle. Keep it factual. Then write one sentence about what you are worth that does not reference a rank, and read that one out loud. That sentence is the one you will need in about four months when this comes back around.

::: prayer
God, my name was not on it and part of me took that as the truth about me. You are the judge and a board is not. Show me what is real in the feedback and let me throw out the verdict I handed myself. Amen.
:::
