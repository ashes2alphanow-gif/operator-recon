# When You Were Wrong

> Confess your sins to one another and pray for one another, that you may be healed. -- James 5:16

A clean admission costs less than every version of covering it. Every time. Without exception.

You know this from the outside. You have watched a man take a small mistake and turn it into a career-ending problem purely through the handling. The original error was survivable. The four months of managing it were not.

You do not know it from the inside, because from the inside the covering always feels cheaper in the moment. It is a five-minute decision made under pressure, and the bill for it arrives in installments over years.

Here is what the covering actually costs. You have to remember the version you gave. You have to avoid a specific person, or a specific topic, indefinitely. You carry a low-grade readiness for it to surface. And every man who suspects the truth has a piece of leverage on you that he did not ask for and may never use, which does not stop it from being leverage.

James does not say confess to God, which is the version most men prefer because it is private and produces no social cost. He says to one another. Out loud, to a person, which is the part that actually breaks the hold, because what a hidden thing runs on is being hidden.

And then he attaches a purpose. So that you may be healed. Not so that you can be punished, or so that the record can be corrected, though sometimes the record does need correcting. The stated goal is that the man gets well.

You have one you have never said. You knew which one before you finished this paragraph.

## THE DRILL

Say it to one human being this week. Pick a man who has no ability to use it against you and no need to. Say it plainly, without the surrounding argument that makes it sound smaller. You do not have to fix anything in the same conversation. Get it out of the dark first, because in the dark it keeps growing and you cannot see how big it actually is.

::: prayer
God, I have been managing something for a long time and the managing costs more than the thing ever did. I am going to say it out loud to a man this week. Keep me from softening it while it is coming out of my mouth. Amen.
:::
