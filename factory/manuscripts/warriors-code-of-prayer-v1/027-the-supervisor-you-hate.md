# The Supervisor You Hate

> If it is possible, as much as it is up to you, be at peace with all men. -- Romans 12:18

You are going to work for him for at least another year.

That is the fact underneath all of this. You cannot fire him, the complaint went nowhere, and the rotation that would move one of you is not on the schedule. Every fantasy you have run about this being resolved is not a plan.

So the only live question is what kind of man you are while you work for him.

There is a cost you may not have counted. Contempt is metabolically expensive. You carry it home. You rehearse conversations in the shower. You give a tenth of your mental bandwidth to a man you do not respect, which is the largest raise he will ever get out of you.

Paul builds two escape hatches into that verse and he builds them on purpose. If it is possible. As much as it is up to you. He is not commanding a result, because the result is not in your control. He is limiting the scope of your responsibility to the part you actually own, which is your conduct.

That is a relief if you take it correctly. You are not obligated to make him reasonable. You are not obligated to feel warm. You are obligated for your half.

Your half looks specific. You do not talk about him to the new guys, because that is where his authority actually gets destroyed and it takes the whole chain with it. You put things in writing, professionally, so that reality has a record. You do your work at a standard that makes his opinion of it irrelevant. And when there is a real integrity issue rather than a personality issue, you take it up the correct channel, once, properly, and let it go where it goes.

## THE DRILL

For one week, do not mention him to anyone junior to you. Not once. Not a joke, not an eye roll. Notice how many times you were about to. That count tells you how much of your day he is renting for free.

::: prayer
God, I hate working for this man and I have let him rent space in my head for two years. I am responsible for my half. Keep my mouth shut in front of the young guys and my work clean enough that his opinion does not matter. Amen.
:::
