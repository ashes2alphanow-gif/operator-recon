# Companion product pass

The paperback is the front door. It is a $6 royalty on a good day. The business
is what sits next to it.

---

For **[TITLE]**, design the companion. Answer in this order.

1. **What does the reader still not have after reading?** Almost always a
   structure to keep doing the thing: a tracker, a set of prompts, a decision
   aid, a set of checklists, a card deck he can run with other men.
2. **Which form?**
   - Printed companion (journal, workbook, card deck) - another KDP or print
     product, same factory, `practice` lane
   - Digital toolkit (spreadsheet, fillable PDF, HTML tool) - highest margin,
     no printing cost, and the thing that gets an email address
   - Institutional version (unit licence, bulk order, a training block built
     around the book) - the highest revenue per hour of work by a wide margin
3. **Price and break-even.** State the fixed spend to build it and the units to
   break even. `./factory/a2a quote` gives you the print side.
4. **What links them?** The book needs a real, working URL to the companion, and
   the companion needs to make sense to somebody who has not read the book.
5. **Synchronisation risk.** If the book cites the companion by page, section,
   or version, both have to move together. Record it in `companion_products` in
   the spec so a later build does not orphan the reference.

Reject any companion that cannot be built in under twenty hours, and any that
requires Chris on camera.
