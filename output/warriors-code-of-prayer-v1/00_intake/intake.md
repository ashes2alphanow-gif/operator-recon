## Reader
- **Who**: Men who work nights and weekends in uniform. Border Patrol, police, fire, medics, corrections, military, and the men who used to and are still carrying it.
- **Problem**: He believes, more or less. He has not prayed a real prayer in months. The devotionals he has been handed were written for a man with a calm week and a quiet house, and they do not survive contact with a night shift.
- **Promise**: Forty short prayers built for a man standing post, written in the language he actually uses.
- **Transformation**: He goes from praying only in emergencies to holding a short, honest, daily position that survives shift work, fatigue, and a job that does not stop.
