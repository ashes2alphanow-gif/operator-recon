# KDP disclosure sheet - A Warrior's Code of Prayer: Forty Days of Prayer for Men Who Work While Others Sleep

Answer the KDP content questions exactly this way:

- **Text**: YES - AI-generated
- **Images**: NO - no AI involvement
- **Translation**: NO - no AI involvement

KDP distinguishes AI-generated content (an AI produced the draft, even if you edited it afterwards) from AI-assisted content (you wrote it, AI helped edit or brainstorm). Only AI-generated content is disclosed. The disclosure is internal to Amazon and is not shown on the product page.
