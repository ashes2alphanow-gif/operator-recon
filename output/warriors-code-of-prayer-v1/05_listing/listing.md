## Description
```
You have not prayed a real prayer in months and you know it. Not because you stopped believing. Because the job ate the hours where that used to happen.

A Warrior's Code of Prayer is a forty-day devotional written for men who work nights, weekends, and holidays in uniform. Border Patrol, police, fire, medics, corrections, military, and the men who used to and are still carrying it.

Every entry runs the same four beats. An anchor line from scripture. A page of plain talk about what that line has to do with the actual job. A drill, which is one thing you do rather than one thing you feel. A prayer short enough to say in a truck with the engine running.

No streaks. No journaling homework. No language that would embarrass you if another guy read it over your shoulder in the briefing room.

WHAT IS INSIDE
- Forty entries, each one finishable in under five minutes
- An anchor, a page, a drill, and a prayer in every entry
- Public domain scripture, so you are not paying for somebody's translation licence
- An index of every anchor line in the back
- 5.5 x 8.5 inches, sized for a cargo pocket or a go-bag

WHO IT IS FOR
- Law enforcement, fire, EMS, corrections, and military
- Veterans and retired first responders still carrying the job
- Fathers who come home at a strange hour and have to be present anyway
- Anyone who wants a devotional that does not sound like it was written for a retreat

WHAT IT IS NOT
This is not therapy, not a theology course, and not a promise that prayer fixes a shift schedule. It is a daily position, written plainly, by a man who works the same hours you do.

Chris G. Stewart. Ashes to Alpha.
```
