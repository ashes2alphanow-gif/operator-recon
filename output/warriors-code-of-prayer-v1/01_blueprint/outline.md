# Build plan - A Warrior's Code of Prayer: Forty Days of Prayer for Men Who Work While Others Sleep

40 entries at ~400 words each = ~16,000 words -> ~102 pages at 5.5x8.5.

Estimated print cost $2.30, floor price $3.84, planned list $13.99, royalty $6.09/copy.

## Outline

1. **Taking the Post** - Showing up on time is the first act of faith, and it counts even when nothing happens.
   - anchor: Psalm 127:1
2. **The Hours Nobody Sees** - Work done where nobody is watching is not wasted work.
   - anchor: Matthew 6:6
3. **Fear on Station** - Courage is fear that took the post anyway, and God never asked you to stop feeling it.
   - anchor: Joshua 1:9
4. **Tired Is Not Weak** - Fatigue is a physical condition, not a character defect, and it needs rest rather than repentance.
   - anchor: Mark 6:31
5. **The Boredom Watch** - Most of the job is nothing happening, and how you hold the nothing decides how you handle the something.
   - anchor: Luke 12:37
6. **Eyes Up** - Vigilance is a discipline you rebuild every shift, not a trait you either have or do not.
   - anchor: 1 Peter 5:8
7. **The Radio Goes Quiet** - Silence from above is not absence, and you already know this from every long night on a dead net.
   - anchor: Psalm 13:1
8. **End of Shift** - How you close a shift decides what you carry into the house.
   - anchor: Psalm 4:8
9. **What You Carry** - You are carrying more than your gear and you have never once written it down.
   - anchor: Matthew 11:28
10. **The Call You Remember** - One call sits on top of the stack for every man, and it does not go away by being ignored.
   - anchor: Psalm 34:18
11. **Anger in the Truck** - Anger is information about something you have not dealt with yet.
   - anchor: Ephesians 4:26
12. **The Guilt You Kept** - Guilt over a decision made in four seconds needs a verdict, not a rerun.
   - anchor: 1 John 1:9
13. **Men Who Did Not Make It** - Grief for the ones who are gone belongs out loud, at least once, to somebody.
   - anchor: John 11:35
14. **The Body Keeps Time** - Your body is keeping a record of this work whether or not you agree to read it.
   - anchor: Psalm 139:14
15. **Drinking It Down** - Every man in this work is offered a cheap way to stop feeling, and it always sends a bill.
   - anchor: Proverbs 20:1
16. **Asking for Help** - Asking for help is a tactical decision, and men who cannot make it get other people hurt.
   - anchor: Galatians 6:2
17. **Walking In the Door** - The first ninety seconds inside your own house set the next twelve hours.
   - anchor: Joshua 24:15
18. **Your Wife Is Not the Enemy** - Threat posture at home reads as contempt, and she is not wrong to name it.
   - anchor: Ephesians 5:25
19. **The Kid Who Waited Up** - Presence is measured in minutes given, not in hours intended.
   - anchor: Deuteronomy 6:7
20. **Teaching a Boy to Stand** - Your son learns what a man does by watching what you do when you are tired.
   - anchor: Proverbs 22:6
21. **What You Say at the Table** - What a family talks about at dinner becomes what the children believe is normal.
   - anchor: Colossians 4:6
22. **The Days You Miss** - You will miss birthdays and games, and how you handle the miss matters more than the miss.
   - anchor: Psalm 90:12
23. **Keeping One Promise** - One promise kept on a bad day outweighs ten made on a good one.
   - anchor: Matthew 5:37
24. **Rest That Is Not Collapse** - Real rest is scheduled and protected, not what is left over after you fall down.
   - anchor: Exodus 20:9
25. **The New Guy** - How you treat the newest man tells the whole crew what the standard actually is.
   - anchor: 1 Timothy 4:12
26. **Leading Tired Men** - Leadership on hour fourteen is mostly about lowering the noise and naming the next task.
   - anchor: Isaiah 40:31
27. **The Supervisor You Hate** - You still have to work for him, and how you do it is a decision about you.
   - anchor: Romans 12:18
28. **Loyalty With a Spine** - Loyalty that cannot say no is not loyalty, it is fear wearing a friendship.
   - anchor: Proverbs 27:6
29. **Getting Passed Over** - A board, a list, or a promotion is not a verdict on your worth as a man.
   - anchor: Psalm 75:6
30. **Keeping Your Word In Writing** - Reports, statements, and logs are where your integrity becomes a record.
   - anchor: Proverbs 12:22
31. **When You Were Wrong** - A clean, fast admission costs less than every version of covering it.
   - anchor: James 5:16
32. **The Man You Trained** - Multiplication is the only thing you build in this job that outlives your tenure.
   - anchor: 2 Timothy 2:2
33. **The Long Career** - Twenty years is a distance event and you cannot sprint it from the front.
   - anchor: Hebrews 12:1
34. **The Body Slows Down** - The day you cannot do what you did at twenty-five is a planning problem, not a shame problem.
   - anchor: 2 Corinthians 4:16
35. **Who You Are Without the Badge** - Identity welded to a job title comes apart on the day the job ends.
   - anchor: Galatians 2:20
36. **Retirement Is a Deployment** - Leaving the work needs a plan with dates in it, the same as any other movement.
   - anchor: Proverbs 21:5
37. **The Man at the Funeral** - Go to the funeral. Stand where people can see you. Say the name out loud.
   - anchor: Romans 12:15
38. **What Gets Written Down** - The doctrine you write outlives you, and most men never write any of it.
   - anchor: Habakkuk 2:2
39. **Handing Off the Post** - A relief briefing is an act of care, and so is everything else you hand the next man.
   - anchor: Numbers 27:18
40. **Standing Down** - You will put this work down one day, and the man who put it down well started practicing early.
   - anchor: 2 Timothy 4:7
