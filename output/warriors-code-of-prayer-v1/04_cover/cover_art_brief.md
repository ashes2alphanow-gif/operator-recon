# Cover art brief - A Warrior's Code of Prayer: Forty Days of Prayer for Men Who Work While Others Sleep

The engine ships a typographic wrap that is print-legal today. Art direction is
Chris's call, not the engine's. If art replaces the flat field, it has to fit
this geometry exactly:

- Full wrap canvas: **11.5500 x 8.7500 in** at 300 DPI
  = **3465 x 2625 px**
- Spine: **0.3000 in** starting 5.6250 in from the left edge
- Front cover panel starts at 5.9250 in
- Keep type 0.25 in inside every trim edge
- Barcode zone stays clear: 2.0 x 1.2 in at (0.38, 0.38) in
- Spine text is NOT allowed at this page count under house rule (min 100 pages)

Thumbnail test before anything else: at 160 px tall the title has to be
readable and the book has to look unlike every other book in the category.

Brand marks: none applied. Ashes to Alpha wordmark is white on black when Chris
says to use it.
