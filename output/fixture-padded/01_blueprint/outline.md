# Build plan - A Season of Stillness: Twenty Reflections for the Journey

20 entries at ~220 words each = ~4,400 words -> ~44 pages at 5.5x8.5.

Estimated print cost $2.30, floor price $3.84, planned list $9.99, royalty $3.69/copy.

## Outline

1. **The Journey Begins** - Starting something you have never done before and surviving the first week of it.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
2. **A Season of Waiting** - What a man does with a stretch of time where nothing is moving.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
3. **The Quiet Voice** - Learning to notice a signal that is quieter than everything around it.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
4. **Letting Go** - Releasing a grudge that has cost more to hold than to drop.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
5. **The Path Ahead** - Choosing a direction when the map runs out at the edge of the paper.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
6. **Trusting the Process** - Staying in a slow process when you would rather force the result.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
7. **A New Perspective** - Looking at an old problem from a position you have never stood in.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
8. **The Inner Work** - The work you do on yourself that nobody else can see or verify.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
9. **Moments of Stillness** - Building a pause into a day that has no room in it.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
10. **Finding Your Center** - Locating the thing you orient on when everything else moves.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
11. **The Deeper Call** - Hearing a request that asks for more than you planned to give.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
12. **Learning to Receive** - Accepting help, money, or praise without deflecting it.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
13. **A Posture of Openness** - Staying open to a person you have already decided about.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
14. **The Gift of Presence** - Giving somebody your attention instead of your advice.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
15. **Walking in Light** - Moving through a place you cannot see the end of.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
16. **The Space Between** - What happens in the gap between a decision and its result.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
17. **Choosing Gratitude** - Naming what is working on a day when little is.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
18. **The Long Obedience** - Doing one unremarkable thing for a very long time.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
19. **Becoming Who You Are** - Growing into the person you already tell other people you are.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
20. **The Final Invitation** - Accepting an offer that will change how the rest of it goes.
   - anchor: Psalm 46:10
