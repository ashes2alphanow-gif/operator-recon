# The Journey Begins

> Be still and know. -- Psalm 46:10

Faith is a posture of the heart rather than a set of actions. When we learn to release control, we begin to experience a deeper sense of peace that is not dependent on circumstances. This is the invitation that is offered to each of us.

In today's world it is important to remember that our journey is a personal one. When it comes to growth, the truth is that we all have a path. At the end of the day, what matters is that we continue to move forward with intention and purpose in all that we do.
