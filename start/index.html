The four tests

Week 1 Monday is your baseline. Week 6 Friday is your retest. Same four events, same order, full rest between.

Push-ups2 minutes, max
Sit-ups2 minutes, max
Pull-upsOne strict set, max
2-mile runFor time

Optional capstone, Week 6 Saturday: 5-mile ruck at 45 lb. Sub-75 is operational. Sub-60, you're back.

Three phases

  • WK 1–2Rebuild. Baseline test, easy volume, get the joints and lungs back on speaking terms.
  • WK 3–4Build. Intervals, heavier strength, longer rucks. This is where it starts to cost something.
  • WK 5–6Peak. Sharpen, taper, retest. Then you know.

The Operator Recon Field Guide

The app runs the program. The Field Guide is what you need when the plan meets your actual week. 48 printable pages:

  • SCALEBeginner and injury substitution tables. What to do instead, and how to log it honestly.
  • TRAVELEquipment, hotel-room and 20-minute versions of every session type, so a bad week doesn't end the program.
  • PRIMERSRucking guide, running and swim primers, warm-up and mobility, recovery, nutrition and meal prep.
  • PHASESA brief for Rebuild, Build and Peak: the intent, the progression rules, the common mistakes.
  • PAPERAll 42 days on paper, baseline/retest/capstone score sheets, weekly logs.
  • WEEK 7+What to do after the retest: a downshift week, then how to pick and write your next block.
